05 — FAQ

Common questions.

Straight answers. No jargon.

Do I need any existing data infrastructure?

Not at all. We start from scratch if needed — designing your entire data stack from the ground up on Google Cloud Platform.

What does "fractional" actually mean?

It means you get senior analyst and data engineer expertise without a full-time salary, benefits, or hiring overhead. You pay for the hours and capacity you actually need.

Who owns the dashboards and models?

You do — 100%. All code, pipelines, and dashboards are yours from day one. There's no vendor lock-in and nothing proprietary on our end.

How long does it take to see results?

Most clients have their first live dashboard within 3–4 weeks of kickoff, depending on the number of data sources and complexity of the build.

Can I scale up or down over time?

Absolutely. That's the whole point. Need more capacity during a product launch or busy quarter? We scale up. Quieter period? Scale back. You're always in control.

What industries do you work with?

We work with any SMB that has data worth understanding — ecommerce, SaaS, professional services, logistics, retail. If you have data, we can make it useful.
